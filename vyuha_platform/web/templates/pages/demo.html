You are never trapped

Pause (or Escape) puts the app back exactly as it was and leaves a small Resume button. Click anything, type anything, answer a question, then carry on where you stopped.

Every action is optional

A step that fills a form offers a button — but you can type it yourself instead if the room would rather watch you do it. Skip with Next.

Wander off freely

Go somewhere the script did not ask for and it says so, and offers the way back, rather than dragging the page away from you. ← and → move between steps.

What the room sees

Each step puts one plain sentence in large type on screen for the audience, with the longer line for you to say underneath it in small type — so nobody is asked to read a paragraph while you talk over it. Every chapter announces itself with a title card and what it proves, and after an action the result stays on screen beside the thing that just changed, instead of a toast that vanishes.
