# Using Vyuha fully

*What a business that gets everything out of it actually does. Read
[What Vyuha does](what-vyuha-does.md) first.*

A tool used at half strength gives half an answer and then gets blamed for it. This is the
rhythm that makes every screen true.

---

## Once, at the start — get the foundation right

Onboarding is run by the Vyuha team in the **Studio**, not left to the client.

1. **Business profile.** Name, owner, WhatsApp number, GSTIN, state, address, bank. Without
   the GSTIN and address, every bill prints as a bill of supply rather than a tax invoice.
2. **The data map.** For each of the eight kinds of record — sales, purchases, stock,
   customers & dues, suppliers & payables, expenses, cash & bank, staff — ask where it lives
   today, how far back it goes, and who keeps it. This decides how history comes in and how
   new data keeps flowing. "Not kept" is a perfectly good answer.
3. **Masters.** Items with their **cost** as well as their price (no cost, no margin), the
   **reorder level** for every item (a level of 0 means Vyuha can never warn you), branches,
   and the people with their **phone numbers and roles** — that is what makes alerts reach
   the right person instead of all landing on the owner.
4. **Cut-over date, opening position and history.** Stock counted, who owes you, whom you
   owe, cash and bank. Before the cut-over is history; after it is the running record.
   *(Stages 4–8 are the next build; today, history goes in as files.)*

**The test of a good setup:** open Finance › Balance sheet and Home on day one. If the
figures are ones the owner recognises, onboarding was done properly.

## Every day — five minutes, twice

**Morning.** Open **Home**. Work down "Needs you" until it is empty:

- *has run out / below reorder* → Order these, then record the delivery when it arrives
- *customers owe you* → Send reminders (the message is already written)
- *supplier bills due* → pay, then mark them paid
- *credit sales with no invoice* → Raise bills
- *register not marked* → mark it; ten seconds, and the month adds up on its own

**As it happens.** Record each sale at the counter (Operations › Record) — or send the
day's file. Take the customer's **WhatsApp number at the moment of sale**; it is the only
moment it is ever to hand, and it is what makes the receipt and the reminder possible.

**Evening.** Glance at Sales › Bills for the day, and at anything sitting in Inbox ›
Waiting.

---

## Every week

- **Collections.** Work the queue top to bottom. Mark "Done" when it is settled and "In a
  week" when they have promised — a chase that arrives after payment costs more than ten
  that work.
- **Inventory.** Everything on "Below reorder" and "Out" is an order to place. Check
  **days of cover** rather than the quantity: twelve pieces is a fortnight of 6205s and two
  years of plummer blocks.
- **Purchases.** Record supplier bills as they arrive, with due dates, so "landing this
  week" on Finance is real.
- **Dead stock.** Anything in "Never sold" is cash on a shelf. Discount it, bundle it or
  return it before it ages further.

---

## Every month

- **Invoice everything.** Any credit sale without an invoice is a customer with a reason
  not to pay. Operations › Invoices groups them by customer.
- **GST.** Finance › GST — tax collected, the estimate of input credit, and the filing
  dates. Send the figures to your CA; do not file from them.
- **The pack.** Finance › Reports — download the PDF and the dashboard, or draft the email
  to your accountant in one click.
- **Read the statements.** P&L, cash flow, balance sheet. The gap between profit and cash
  is the number that matters: profit you have not collected is not money you can spend.
- **Check the ratios** (Analytics › Ratios): debtor days, stock days, the cash cycle, and
  whether one customer carries too much of the business.

## Set it up to nag you

These are what turn Vyuha from a screen you must remember into something that reaches you.

- **Reorder levels on every item** — Settings › Stock levels. This is the single highest
  return setting in the product.
- **Everybody's phone number and role** — Team › Staff. Vyuha tells whoever owns the
  problem; a person with no number is skipped silently.
- **Check "Who gets told"** — Inbox › Who gets told shows who would hear what, before
  anybody hears it.
- **A morning routine** — Inbox › Routines. An 8 am brief means the owner starts the day
  knowing what needs him without opening anything.
- **Dead-stock and cover thresholds** — Settings › Business, if this trade turns slower or
  faster than most.

## Ask, instead of building reports

Anything Analytics › Explore can do, the assistant can do in words — and it can do the
things Explore cannot:

- "Who owes me the most, and since when?"
- "What did Nandi Sugars buy last quarter?"
- "Which items have not moved in ninety days?"
- "Compare this month with the same month last year."
- "Make a deck for the bank."

Every figure in the answer is computed from the books. If an answer surprises you, open the
screen behind it — they cannot disagree.

---

## What "using it fully" looks like after three months

- Home opens with **two or three** items, not fifteen — the list is being worked.
- Debtor days are falling, because reminders go out the day a bill ages.
- Nothing is ever out of stock without somebody having been told first.
- Every credit sale has an invoice against it, and GST is a half-hour at month end.
- The owner has not opened a spreadsheet to answer a question in weeks.
