**The cash cycle is the headline for a bank or an investor.** Negative means
customers pay him roughly 25 days before his suppliers need paying. The business
funds itself out of its own trading.

## Tax

Three GST rates across the catalogue — **0% on seed, 5% on fertiliser, 18% on
tools and hardware** — so a three-line invoice needs a real tax summary, not one
flat rate. CGST + SGST within Karnataka, IGST outside it.
