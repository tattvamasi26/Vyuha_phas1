# How Vyuha talks

*Paste this for anything a customer will read.*

## The voice in one line

Plain, specific, and slightly understated. The way a good accountant explains
something — not the way software markets itself.

## Six rules

**1. Lead with the number.**
> "Ramu Stores owes the most — ₹83,200, overdue 66 days."

Not: *"Looking at your receivables, we can see that there are some outstanding
amounts that may require attention."*

**2. Concrete nouns from their trade.**
Bags, godown, bill, party, reorder, shelf. Not: units, warehouse, invoice
document, entity, inventory threshold.

**3. Say what it cannot do, in the same breath as what it can.**
> "Cash is receipts minus payments recorded here — not a bank balance."

This is a feature of the voice, not a disclaimer. It is what makes the rest
believable.

**4. Short sentences. One idea each.**
If a sentence has a comma and then a "which", split it.

**5. Rupees Indian-style.** ₹54,05,800 — not ₹5,405,800 and not ₹5.4M.
Rounded on a slide: ₹5.4 crore, ₹2.18 lakh.

**6. Never sell the software. Show what it found.**
"Three items have never sold — ₹34,190 sitting on your shelf" does more work
than any sentence containing the word "insights".

## Banned words

platform · solution · leverage · streamline · empower · seamless · robust ·
cutting-edge · revolutionise · unlock · supercharge · game-changing ·
best-in-class · end-to-end · holistic · synergy · AI-powered

If a sentence needs one of these, the sentence has no content yet.

## Words to use instead

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| platform / solution | Vyuha, or "it" |
| leverage AI | reads, works out, answers |
| actionable insights | what needs doing |
| streamline operations | save him an hour a day |
| seamless integration | send the file as it is |
| real-time visibility | he can see it now |
| optimise inventory | stop money sitting on the shelf |

## Tone by document

| Document | Tone |
|---|---|
| **Brochure** | Warm, plain, one clear promise. Almost no adjectives. |
| **Deck** | Terse. One idea per slide. The number is the sentence. |
| **Case study** | Narrative — before, what changed, after. Real figures throughout. |
| **Bank file** | Formal, complete, careful. State every assumption. |
| **WhatsApp** | Six lines maximum. He is reading it standing up. |
| **Invoice** | No voice at all. It is a legal document. |

## A worked example

**Wrong:**
> Vyuha is an AI-powered platform that empowers distributors with real-time,
> actionable insights to streamline inventory management and optimise cash flow.

**Right:**
> Send Vyuha the stock file you already keep. It will tell you what is about to
> run out, what has been sitting for ninety days, and who owes you money.

Same claim. The second one someone might actually believe.

## The line that sells it

> "Send us your file. Don't clean it up first — that's the point."
